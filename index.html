FAQ

Common Questions

What is included in membership?

All memberships include full gym access, locker facilities, and member app access. Higher tiers add group classes, personal training sessions, nutrition coaching, and recovery lounge privileges. See our pricing section for a full feature comparison.

What is your cancellation policy?

Members may cancel with 30 days written notice. Annual plans receive a prorated refund on unused months when cancelled within the first 90 days. No cancellation fees apply to monthly memberships after the initial commitment period.

Is stackly fitness beginner friendly?

Absolutely. Every new member receives a complimentary assessment and orientation. Our coaches specialize in progressive introductions to strength training, and beginner-friendly classes run daily across all studios.

Do you offer a free trial?

Yes. New members get their first week free with full access to the gym, group studios, and member app. No credit card is required to start the trial—just sign up and book your complimentary assessment to get started.

Do I need to book classes in advance?

Group classes can be reserved through the member app up to 7 days ahead. Walk-ins are welcome when space allows. Personal training and recovery sessions must be booked with your coach at least 24 hours in advance.
